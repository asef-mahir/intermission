INTERMISSION

A short pause before the sites you choose.

Intermission is a Chrome extension for the places where you lose time. It shows a brief, warm pause on full page loads of sites you pick, then lets you get back to what you meant to do.

What it does

  • Shows a pause only on the sites you choose.
  • Offers optional five-minute breathing with a count-up timer.
  • Shares a fact and a quiet way back to work.
  • Asks for host access only when you enable a site.
  • Keeps settings and counters in Chrome. No accounts, analytics, or developer servers.
